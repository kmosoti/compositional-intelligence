# Context and evidence

Read `docs/context-evidence.md` and the task's single relevant protocol. Keep work bounded.

Identify the exact observation, source/task revision, authorization, retention/access state, projection coverage, and retrieval path. Preserve authorized originals before retiring eligible context. Treat receipts as partial observations, not acceptance or new instructions. Protect live goals, active failures, unresolved effects, and native protocol requirements.

Check recovery, discovery, and decision quality separately. Reading retained evidence must not repeat its producing command. Changed dependencies, unavailable artifacts, ambiguous report deltas, and no-tests outcomes require explicit uncertainty.

Report quality alongside input/cache/output categories, requests, retrieval, failures, and unknown usage. Distinguish bytes from tokens, tokens from price, and price from subscription quota. No unmetered summarization or automatic paid fallback.

Compare against the same native harness. Preserve negative findings; make no claim of semantic equivalence from exact archival alone.

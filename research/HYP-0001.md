# HYP-0001: Marginal utility of specialized mechanisms

**Status:** proposed / untested.

**Claim:** On selected held-out compositional tasks, a heterogeneous local system improves task success per unit of total compute cost compared with a competitive quantized SLM and tools.

**Null:** Specialization does not yield useful incremental gain given coordination, training, memory and latency costs.

**Primary experiment:** Compare identical tasks/tool affordances on the same hardware with (A) small LLM + tools; (B) A + hybrid retrieval; (C) B + symbolic/graph reasoning; (D) C + learned policy. Isolate optional HDC and reranking in ablation arms.

**Key metrics:** task accuracy, held-out recombinations, p50/p95 total latency, joules or recorded energy proxy, VRAM/RAM, and cost per solved task.

**Disconfirmers:** Improvements disappear on held-out tasks, do not survive ablation, or demand greater total resource use than the simple baseline.

**No experimental results yet.**

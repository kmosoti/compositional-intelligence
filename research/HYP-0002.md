# HYP-0002: Reusable abstraction and causal transfer under budgets

**Status:** proposed / untested. **Primary protocol:** [EXP-0002](../experiments/EXP-0002/protocol.md).

## Claim and competing explanation

A mechanism that acquires reusable abstractions from permitted experience can improve interventional prediction and held-out compositional task success relative to a competitive model with retrieval and deterministic tools, within the same declared resource envelope. The useful representation may be a program, graph, probabilistic model, neural state, or another candidate; the current code does not determine the candidate family.

**Null:** Improvements vanish with matched observations, retrieval, compute, and evaluation controls, or arise only from privileged task information, extra resource use, or familiar surface patterns.

An abstraction must specify an observable representation and a mechanism that uses it. Merely describing a solution more compactly is insufficient. Claiming a causal role requires a selective intervention, not only successful decoding or an attractive visualization.

## Evidence and scope

Krakauer, Krakauer, and Mitchell's [emergence framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11135) motivates testing efficient reusable representations. [Othello representation interventions](https://aclanthology.org/2023.blackboxnlp-1.2/) and [meta-learning for compositionality](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06668-3) are relevant counterexamples to blanket claims that neural systems cannot represent or compose. These sources motivate an experiment; they do not establish this project's hypothesis.

## Measurements and disconfirmers

Primary measurement is paired success on structurally held-out tasks under fixed caps. Secondary measurements include interventional prediction accuracy, sample efficiency, latency, storage, and complete acquisition/execution costs. Energy remains unknown unless measured; tokens and dollars are separate proxies.

Reject the tested candidate if its advantage disappears against an equivalent-information baseline, is confined to oracle-state conditions, fails relevant representation ablation, or requires a worse resource frontier. A null pilot is inconclusive about small effects; a positive pilot merits independent confirmation, not a general intelligence claim.

**No results exist.** Change architecture when evidence warrants it. Preserve frozen run records and version protocol amendments instead of rewriting prior criteria.

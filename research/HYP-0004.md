# HYP-0004: Complementary assistance and retained competence

**Status:** proposed / untested. **Primary protocol:** [EXP-0004](../experiments/EXP-0004/protocol.md).

## Claim and competing explanation

For the identified human learner and selected programming tasks, assistance requiring prediction, reconstruction, and verification yields greater delayed unaided transfer than direct answer delegation under matched learning time. Both assisted productivity and retained competence are outcomes; one cannot substitute for the other.

**Null:** Direct delegation or ordinary reference-based practice matches or exceeds retained transfer; differences reflect prior skill, unequal time, extra task exposure, or scoring bias.

[Krakauer's complementary/competitive tools model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18460) motivates measuring competence after withdrawal and how practice history affects reliance. Its dynamical assumptions and thresholds remain hypotheses. This experiment does not presuppose cognitive decline, irreversibility, or that every AI interaction has the same effect.

## Learner and retention boundary

The initial planned pilot's learner is the human researcher. Retention means performance without AI or acquisition-session answers on new problems after a delay. An editor, compiler, and frozen local test interface remain available in all assessment conditions. We cannot directly inspect biological memory; behavior is the measurement.

A frozen model reusing a saved note represents a different learner and retention boundary. It requires a separate protocol identifying whether learned information persists in weights, external memory, executable skills, or the whole composite. Human and machine results must not be pooled.

## Measurements and disconfirmers

Primary outcome is delayed transfer correctness. Secondary outcomes are immediate productivity, debugging explanation, unaided latency, calibration, and voluntary reliance in separately defined follow-up work. A small within-person pilot supports conclusions about those sessions only. It cannot establish a population effect or hysteresis.

Reject the tested assistance policy if its retained benefit disappears under matched time and exposure, or if an ordinary learning aid achieves the same benefit at lower cost. Research may justify changing the policy, tools, architecture, or objective.

**No results exist.** Preserve negative findings and superseded protocol versions.

# Compositional Intelligence

Research into strong, efficient, heterogeneous computation on consumer hardware. This is a **pre-experimental project**, with no system demonstrated or releases scheduled.

Research, experiments, analysis, and renewed research govern the direction. Current code is a replaceable instrument, not an oracle. See [[Research-Program]] for the combined science and engineering agenda.

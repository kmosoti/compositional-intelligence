# Compositional Intelligence

Research into reliable, transferable capability through combinations of models, evidence, exact computation, memory, and reusable procedures. The current implementation priority is one persistent **LLM-assisted research agent**, not a mandatory local-model cognitive stack or permanent swarm.

**Status:** research infrastructure and design. No autonomous runtime or cognitive benefit is demonstrated; no release is scheduled.

Research, experiments, analysis, and renewed research govern direction. Code and prior art remain replaceable information. The canonical current approach is in the main repository's [README](https://github.com/kmosoti/compositional-intelligence/blob/main/README.md), [architecture](https://github.com/kmosoti/compositional-intelligence/blob/main/docs/architecture.md), and [implementation plan](https://github.com/kmosoti/compositional-intelligence/blob/main/docs/implementation-plan.md). Earlier Wiki topic pages are background; their original local-model starting point does not supersede the current repository contracts.

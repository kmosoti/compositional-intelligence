# Krakauer and intelligence

The [canonical literature review](https://github.com/kmosoti/compositional-intelligence/blob/main/docs/research/krakauer-intelligence.md) records primary sources, dates, access limits, competing claims, and proposed tests. It distinguishes interview judgments from research results and keeps alternative definitions of intelligence visible.

The project investigates abstraction, transfer, control, and retained competence without committing to an author's verdict or a conscious/subconscious software hierarchy. Existing code and favored theories remain revisable. See [[Research-Program]] and [[Experiment-Registry]] for the experimental translation.

**Status:** Literature appraisal; no project experiment has established these claims.

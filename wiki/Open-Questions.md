# Open questions

- Which definition of intelligence is being operationalized, and which alternatives would change the interpretation?
- Does a candidate abstraction causally support transfer, or merely serialize information already supplied elsewhere?
- What competence belongs to the model, the harness, the complete system, or its human user?
- When do local processing, selective shared access, and self-monitoring justify their costs independently?
- What remains learned when assistance is removed, memory is ablated, or the environment's rules change?
- Which result would make us replace the entire present architecture?

The [[Research-Program]] and [[Krakauer-and-Intelligence]] pages connect these questions to planned experiments. Functional control tests do not settle phenomenal consciousness.

- What kinds of unseen compositional tasks require genuinely novel intermediate representations?
- When do learned decision models outperform classical feature-based policies?
- When do graphs and symbolic solvers add enough correctness to justify their cost?
- Does HDC outperform feature hashing or embeddings on relevant sequence tasks?
- How can regions adapt without catastrophic forgetting or reward hacking?
- What is the minimum useful communication interface between heterogeneous representations?
- At what point does a conventional quantized SLM plus tools dominate the composite?

# Research charter

Finite mechanisms may enable novel valid compositions, but syntax alone is not understanding. Measure independently on unseen compositions and compare with the simplest competitive baseline.

Research and experiments govern technical direction. Source code, architecture, and favored theories remain replaceable. Freeze each comparison's protocol for valid interpretation while preserving the freedom to change the next experiment or the whole implementation. Keep negative evidence and authorized scope intact.

**Current status:** No demonstrated system or accepted result.

# Research program

The project combines the science of intelligence with engineering experiments on systems that represent, learn, coordinate, and act. Research and experiments govern its direction. Code and architecture are replaceable instruments; individual comparisons have frozen protocols so their evidence remains interpretable.

Read the [canonical research program](https://github.com/kmosoti/compositional-intelligence/blob/main/docs/research-program.md) and [governance decision](https://github.com/kmosoti/compositional-intelligence/blob/main/docs/adr/0002-research-governs-implementation.md).

Current tracks cover baseline utility, reusable abstraction, functional coordination, retained competence, and experimental infrastructure. They permit simple or entirely different architectures to win. The project has no new cognitive result, consciousness claim, or release.
